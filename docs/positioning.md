# Brazework: positioning research (2026-11-07)

**Bottom line:** the opening is about *trust*, not a nicer LinkedIn.
- AI has flooded hiring with noise: about 3x the applications per role, AI-written posts, and fake candidates.
- Entry-level STEM jobs have gotten harder to land.
- Verified STEM people are now worth money. Handshake and Mercor earn most of their revenue selling expert time to AI labs.
- The "LinkedIn but better" startups (Polywork, Read.cv) both shut down in January 2025.

**Brazework should start as the verified proof-of-work network for university STEM clubs, one club and one campus at a time.**

Claims marked *(unverified)* couldn't be confirmed against a primary source.

## 1. Market

- **US STEM workforce:** 36M in 2023, 25% of all workers. 24M of them hold a STEM degree, which is the more relevant total market ([NSF/NCSES 2026](https://ncses.nsf.gov/pubs/nsb20261/stem-labor)).
- **US STEM bachelor's degrees:** about 435,500 a year, 22% of all bachelor's degrees ([NCES](https://nces.ed.gov/programs/coe/indicator/cta)).
- **Long-term demand:** BLS projects STEM jobs to grow 8.1% from 2024 to 2034, against 2.7% for non-STEM. The median STEM wage is $103,580 ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/opub/ted/2025/numbers-research-and-discovery-stem-employment-projected-to-take-off.htm)).
- **Entry level is squeezed.** Unemployment for recent graduates is 6.1% in computer science and 7.5% in computer engineering ([NY Fed via CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/05/16/college-majors-with-the-best-and-worst-employment-prospects.html)). Workers aged 22–25 in jobs most exposed to AI saw a 13% relative drop in employment ([Stanford](https://siepr.stanford.edu/publications/working-paper/canaries-coal-mine-six-facts-about-recent-employment-effects-artificial)).
- **Referrals still win.** They're about 1% of applications but 40% of them reach an interview ([Ashby](https://www.ashbyhq.com/talent-trends-report/reports/referrals)). Don't quote "85% of jobs come from networking": it traces back to a single 2016 LinkedIn post *(unverified)*.

## 2. Competitors and gaps

| Platform | What's missing for STEM |
|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** (1.2B+ members) | Noise: about 54% of 2025 long-form posts from top profiles were likely AI-written ([Originality.ai via PPC Land](https://ppc.land/over-half-of-linkedin-posts-are-now-likely-ai-but-authenticity-still-wins/)), and recruiter spam is a constant complaint. **Caveat:** LinkedIn has 100M+ verified members ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/18/linkedins-profile-verification-push-is-accelerating-and-india-is-leading-the-charge-in-2025)), so a verification badge alone is not a moat. |
| **Handshake** | **The closest threat.** It already has a student feed and Communities ([updates](https://updates.joinhandshake.com/announcements/handshake-student-product-updates-april-11-2025)). But about $1B of its ~$1.15B revenue run rate is now AI-training work ([Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/127345-handshakes-arr-crosses-1b-as-ai-training-revenue-surges/)). It is run by career centers, not by clubs. |
| **GitHub / Kaggle / Devpost** | Code- or competition-only. No hardware, bio or chem work, and no persistent professional graph. |
| **ResearchGate** | Built around papers. Not for students or industry. |
| **Polywork** | Raised $44.5M, then shut down Jan 2025. It was a broad LinkedIn alternative without an atomic network. |
| **Read.cv** | Acquired by Perplexity and wound down in Jan 2025 ([TechCrunch via Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/perplexity-acquires-read-cv-social-202751374.html)). |
| **Discord / Slack** | **Where clubs live today.** Chat disappears, can't be searched, and carries no identity or career value. **Brazework replaces this.** |

## 3. Why now

- **Hiring trust has broken down.** 91% of recruiters have caught candidate deception (the "3x applications since 2021" figure isn't on this page; removed from the site 2026-11-09) ([Greenhouse](https://www.greenhouse.com/newsroom/an-ai-trust-crisis-70-of-hiring-managers-trust-ai-to-make-faster-and-better-hiring-decisions-only-8-of-job-seekers-call-it-fair)).
- **Fake candidates are coming.** Gartner expects 1 in 4 candidate profiles to be fake by 2028 ([HR Dive](https://www.hrdive.com/news/fake-job-candidates-ai/757126/)).
- **Skills are replacing résumés.** 85% of employers say they use skills-based hiring ([TestGorilla](https://www.testgorilla.com/skills-based-hiring/state-of-skills-based-hiring-2025/)).
- **Verified STEM expertise has a cash price.** Mercor is valued at $10B ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/27/mercor-quintuples-valuation-to-10b-with-350m-series-c/)).

## 4. Positioning

**One-liner:** Brazework is where STEM builders show verified work, not résumés: the home for your club, your projects, and the people who'll hire you.

**Wedge:** university STEM club leaders: robotics, rocketry, ACM/IEEE, hackathon organizers, SWE/NSBE/SHPE. Win one campus deeply before the next ([PG, *Do Things That Don't Scale*](https://paulgraham.com/ds.html)).

**Atomic network:** the smallest network that is useful on its own ([Andrew Chen](https://techcrunch.com/2021/12/08/andrew-chen-of-a16z-on-how-startups-get-past-a-cold-start-to-survive-and-thrive)). For Brazework, that's one club of 30–100 members plus its sponsors and events.

**Core loop:**
1. A club runs its events and community on Brazework.
2. Members post projects and results tied to the club or event.
3. Verified sponsors and alumni see the work and follow or message.
4. Members gain opportunities.
5. Members bring in the next club.

**Defensible differentiators:**
1. **Attested proof of work.** A verified club, school or company vouches for the project, not just for the person's identity.
2. **A club operating system with a career graph.** Switching costs build up at the organization level.
3. **Trust and safety by design.** Verified orgs, a request inbox with block and report, and analytics that are private by default.

## 5. Business model

- **Students and clubs:** free.
- **First revenue:** company sponsorship of club events and challenges, the budget campus recruiters already spend. Benchmarks: LinkedIn Recruiter Lite ~$170/mo ([Dover](https://www.dover.com/blog/linkedin-recruiter-lite-vs-professional)); Handshake Pro ~$450/mo *(unverified)*.
- **Later:** individual premium (benchmark: LinkedIn Premium $29.99/mo), then a verified-expert marketplace.

## 6. What YC will look at

- **Growth:** 5–7% a week is good and 10% is exceptional ([PG, *Startup = Growth*](https://paulgraham.com/growth.html)).
- **Retention:** consumer social is good at ~25% after six months and great at ~45% ([Lenny Rachitsky](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/what-is-good-retention-issue-29)).
- **Metrics to show:**
  - Weekly active users who post, RSVP or message.
  - The share of a pilot club's members who are weekly active.
  - The number of clubs running their events on Brazework.
  - Messages from organizations to students.
  - Clubs referred by other clubs.
- **Headwind:** YC expects founders to go full-time ([FAQ](https://www.ycombinator.com/faq)). Agree on a go/no-go trigger, for example 3 clubs above 40% weekly-active for 8 weeks.

## 7. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Cold start (what killed Polywork and Read.cv) | One campus above 50% weekly-active club members before expanding. Make Brazework useful to a single club with zero outside network. |
| Handshake | Go in through clubs, not career centers. Include professionals and alumni, whom Handshake doesn't serve. |
| Verification is copyable | The moat is attested work plus club history. Tighten the school check, which today is a domain pattern only. |
| Part-time founders | A clear trigger metric for going full-time. Ruthless scope. |
| Trust and safety at student scale | Move auto-moderation into the database, build a report queue, and give club admins moderation tools. |
