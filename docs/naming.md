# Renaming Forge (2026-11-10)

## Decision: Brazework, pending a trademark check

**Brazework.** Brazing joins two metals into one piece stronger than either alone. That's what Forge does for students, their clubs and companies: verified work holds them together. "Verified on Brazework." Josh picked it on 2026-11-10. Signet, the earlier frontrunner, was dropped: signet.com, .app and .io are all registered, and Signet Jewelers is a large company.

**The risk to clear first:** Braze, Inc. (NASDAQ: BRZE, customer-engagement software) holds about 24 US trademarks on BRAZE, including BRAZEAI (2024, AI software). We'd also be software. Search BRAZE and BRAZEWORK at tmsearch.uspto.gov in classes 9, 35, 41 and 42. A flat-fee clearance opinion from a trademark attorney (about $300–500) is worth it before we file our own mark. Tracked in Linear **FIX-26**; the company setup that follows (domain, email, accounts) is **FIX-57**.

**Not renamed:** table names like `forge_admins`, env var prefixes and the repo name stay as they are unless we decide otherwise (FIX-35).

The rest of this page is the first shortlist, kept for the reasoning.

## Why rename

- **"Forge" is crowded in software**, which makes it hard to search for, hard to trademark and easy to confuse: Autodesk Forge, Atlassian Forge, Laravel Forge, SourceForge, Forge (Minecraft mods) and many more.
- **The "proof" names are taken too.** Direct competitors already use them: Proofworks ("Proof, Not Pedigree"), D'Proof, Proof CV, Skallery ("verifiable proof of professional ability"), BloomNow ("verified Skills Passport"), Provio. A "Proof…" name would blend in.
- **What only we have is the club.** Others verify with tests, AI or teachers (SkillStory is "teacher-attested", SkillGraph uses "faculty-backed project verification"). On Forge, **your club's officers, who saw the work happen, put their name on it**, and companies find you through that. The name should point there: a seal, a sign-off, a mark from people who were there.

## What a good name does here

1. Says, or hints at, *vouched-for work*: a seal, a mark, a sign-off.
2. Works for students, club officers and recruiters alike, and reads well in "Verified on ___".
3. Is short, easy to spell after hearing it once, and ownable (search, domain, trademark in the software/education classes).

## Shortlist

| Name | The idea | Fit | What a quick search found |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Signet** | A signet is the seal that authenticates a document. Your club's signet on your work. "Verified on Signet." | Strong meaning, premium feel, one word | No student or careers product found. Signet Jewelers and Signet Bank exist in other industries, so a trademark check in software/education classes is needed. |
| **Vouchd** | The action itself, made ownable by the spelling | Very clear, verb-ready ("get vouchd") | "Vouch" alone is crowded (VouchMe, Vouch freelance, Vouchcard, Vochure). The spelling helps, but sits close to them. |
| **Benchmark** (rejected) | Proven against a standard | Generic; impossible to own | n/a |
| **Signoff** | Officers sign off on milestones | Plain-English and accurate | An enterprise AI startup launched as "Signoff" in 2026, so it's taken in software. |
| **Kiln, Mettle, Crucible, Attest, Clubmark** (rejected) | Forge-adjacent or verification words | | All taken in adjacent markets: Kiln (crypto staking, and a future-of-work company), Mettle (NatWest bank, a hiring app), Crucible (careers institute), Attest (consumer research), Clubmark (UK sports-club accreditation). |

**First recommendation (superseded by Brazework, above): Signet**, if it clears a trademark check. It's the only candidate that carries our actual difference (an authority you trust stamps the work) and isn't already used by a careers or education product. Fallback: keep **Forge** as the company and use a distinct product mark, e.g. "Forge Signet" for the verified badge, until a rename is clear.

## Before committing (an afternoon, not code)

1. USPTO trademark search (tmsearch.uspto.gov) for the name in classes 9, 35, 41 and 42 (software, hiring, education, SaaS).
2. Domain: the .com if possible; otherwise a short .co, .app or getname.com. Check handles on Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn and X.
3. Say it to three students and one recruiter, without explaining it, and ask what they think it does.
