Brazework · how it works

Proof, not résumés.

Students do real work in clubs and study groups. On Brazework, someone who saw it happen signs it, and companies hire from that proof.

The loop

Everything in the app serves one of four steps.

1 · Join

Your club, team or study group runs on Brazework: members, events, chat.

2 · Build

Post a project and its milestones as you go, with photos, video and numbers.

3 · Get it signed

An officer, your group or a professor who saw it vouches. Their name stays on it.

4 · Get found

Companies search by signed work, message you and sponsor your club.

The app

Five places, one question each.

PlaceThe questionWhat you do thereLoop step
TodayWhat needs me right now?Your one next step, your week, the next event, then posts from your clubs and people you follow.gets you moving
BuildWhat am I making?Your projects. Add one, post updates, mark milestones and ask for them to be signed.2
ClubWhat's my team doing?Clubs and study groups: chat, events, members. Officers sign members' work here.1 · 3
ProofWhat have I proven, and who looked?Your public profile. Signed work first, who signed it, which companies viewed you.3 · 4
InboxWho's reaching out?Messages from members and from companies.4

Ember, the AI helper, works inside all five. It drafts posts, club updates and recaps; a person always approves before anything goes out.

One week, three seats

The same loop, seen by each person in it.

Student · Maya

  1. Build: adds "Line-following robot" and posts a photo update.
  2. Build: marks "Beat the course in 41 s" and asks Robotics Club to sign it.
  3. Proof: it shows as verified, signed by the president.
  4. Inbox: a sponsor company messages her.

Club officer · Ben

  1. Club: runs events, chat and announcements; Ember drafts the busywork.
  2. Club → Verify: signs members' milestones he saw happen.
  3. Club → This week: gets a report of what the club built, to show sponsors.

Company · a sponsor

  1. Searches students by signed work, like "embedded systems, verified".
  2. Opens profiles and messages the ones it likes.
  3. Sponsors the club whose members did the work.
The business

Students never pay. Companies pay first, universities pay most.

Free

Students, clubs and study groups. They create the proof, so they never pay to be on Brazework.

Companies

Search by signed work, and club sponsorships with a report of what the club built. The first revenue.

Universities, later

Every club and study group on one campus, with the outcomes. Once five or more clubs are live at a school.

The rule for every feature

Does it help work get done, get signed, or get seen?

If yes, it belongs. If no, it's noise on the first screen people see.

  • belongsStudy groups: members sign who led them, and it lands on the leader's Proof.
  • belongsThe next event as one line on Today.
  • noiseOutside news headlines, and trending tags at today's size.
